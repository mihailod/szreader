IMPORTANT
Before any App Store build: deploy the CloudKit schema to Production in the CloudKit Console — record types Issue and Reading. The schema auto-creates in Development only, so your dev build will keep working perfectly while the shipped one talks to an empty database. This is the single easiest way to ship a broken sync, so I've written it into my notes as well as saying it here.
