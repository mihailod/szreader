A milestone in the cavalcade of Comics Code changes during the switchover from pre-Code to approved comics. The story about "Stick" combines truly awful art with countless edits, missing balloons, muzzle flashes without any muzzle, hastily re-lettered dialogue, and black-and-white panels.
