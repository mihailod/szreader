Vintage SOFTALK Magazine Library

SOFTALK Magazine was an early 80's Apple II focused magazine that was
published from September 1980 to August 1984. Here for your reading pleasure
are the complete archives, scanned, restored, and donated by Steve M., who also
restored our MacWorld and MacUser archives.
