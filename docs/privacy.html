StreamZine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

StreamZine has no account system, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and no server of its own. It never sees your StripZona username or password. Everything it keeps stays on your iPad.

What the app stores, and where

The app ships empty. It contains no library, no issues and no artwork — a fresh install has nothing in it at all. Everything below exists only because you put it there, by importing a page or downloading an issue, and only ever inside the app's own container on your device:

  • the library — titles, issue numbers, series, publishers and the links read off the pages you import;
  • issues you download, and the pages unpacked from them;
  • cover images, cached so they are not fetched twice;
  • where you stopped reading, what you have marked read, and your layout and filter preferences.

None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Signing in to StripZona

Some posts hide their links until you are signed in. You sign in on StripZona's own form, shown inside the app in a system web view — the same WebKit component Safari is built on.

The app never sees your credentials. It has no login screen of its own, no field of its own to type into, and nothing that watches what you type. What you enter goes from StripZona's form to StripZona. Your username and password are never read, never stored, never sent anywhere by the app, and nothing is written to the Keychain.

The session cookie StripZona sets is held by the system web view inside the app's container, which is why you stay signed in between launches and why removing the app removes it.

Network requests

The app connects only where you direct it:

  • stripzona.com — browsing the forum and importing a page;
  • stripovi.com — cover artwork that forum posts link to;
  • the file host a post links to — MediaFire, Mega or Pixeldrain — when you tap Download.

Those are ordinary web requests, so those sites see what any website sees: your IP address and the request itself. They are independent services under their own privacy policies, not this one. The app sends them no information about you beyond the request.

Importing a page

When you tap Import, the app reads the HTML of the page you are looking at, so it can pick out the issue titles, numbers and links. That happens only when you tap Import, only for the page on screen, and the result stays on your device. The app does not read pages you have not asked it to import, and does not run anything while you browse.

Content

StreamZine is a reader. It hosts no files, ships no issues, and publishes nothing. It shows what is already on the forum and downloads only what you ask it to.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It shows whatever the forum topics you import contain, which is not moderated by this app.
